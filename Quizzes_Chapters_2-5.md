# Chapter Quizzes: Chapters 2–5

These quizzes review the core ideas from Chapters 2–5. Each chapter quiz contains 20 items. Choose the best answer for each item. Unless a question asks you to identify more than one feature, select one answer. The answer keys include brief explanations so you can review the reasoning as well as the correct form.

## Chapter 2 Quiz: Nouns

### Questions

1. Which word is a noun in this sentence? *The technician repaired the printer.*  
   A. the  
   B. repaired  
   C. technician  
   D. quickly

2. Which is a proper noun?  
   A. university  
   B. professor  
   C. building  
   D. Pacific Ocean

3. Which noun is abstract?  
   A. courage  
   B. suitcase  
   C. harbor  
   D. nurse

4. Which noun is noncount in standard usage?  
   A. chair  
   B. information  
   C. suggestion  
   D. document

5. Which sentence uses *advice* correctly?  
   A. She gave me an advice.  
   B. She gave me several advice.  
   C. She gave me some advice.  
   D. She gave me two advices.

6. What is the standard plural of *city*?  
   A. citys  
   B. cities  
   C. cityes  
   D. citties

7. What is the standard plural of *box*?  
   A. boxs  
   B. boxies  
   C. boxes  
   D. boxen

8. What is the standard plural of *child*?  
   A. childs  
   B. children  
   C. childes  
   D. childrens

9. Which sentence has the correct plural form?  
   A. The two womans arrived early.  
   B. The two women arrived early.  
   C. The two womens arrived early.  
   D. The two woman arrived early.

10. Which is the correct possessive form for one student?  
    A. the students notebook  
    B. the student's notebook  
    C. the students' notebook  
    D. the student notebook's

11. Which is the correct possessive form for several employees?  
    A. the employee's schedules  
    B. the employees schedules  
    C. the employees' schedules  
    D. the employees's schedules

12. In *The manager approved the budget*, what is the subject noun?  
    A. manager  
    B. budget  
    C. approved  
    D. the

13. In *We reviewed the proposal*, which noun is the direct object?  
    A. We  
    B. reviewed  
    C. proposal  
    D. the

14. In *Mina became a supervisor*, what role does *supervisor* play?  
    A. subject  
    B. direct object  
    C. subject complement  
    D. possessive noun

15. Which word should be capitalized because it is part of a proper noun?  
    A. river, in *the river is high*  
    B. monday, in *our meeting is monday*  
    C. office, in *the office is open*  
    D. student, in *the student called*

16. Which sentence correctly uses a count noun with a quantity word?  
    A. We received much applications.  
    B. We received many applications.  
    C. We received an applications.  
    D. We received few application.

17. Which phrase correctly expresses a quantity of *equipment*?  
    A. three equipments  
    B. many equipment  
    C. three pieces of equipment  
    D. an equipment

18. Which pair contains a concrete noun followed by an abstract noun?  
    A. desk; patience  
    B. honesty; kindness  
    C. manager; student  
    D. freedom; bicycle

19. Choose the correct plural possessive: The feedback came from several ____.  
    A. customer  
    B. customer's  
    C. customers  
    D. customers'

20. Choose the best correction: *The informations are in the folder.*  
    A. The information is in the folder.  
    B. The informations is in the folder.  
    C. The information are in the folder.  
    D. The informationes are in the folder.

### Answer key

1. **C — technician.** It names a person.  
2. **D — Pacific Ocean.** It is the name of a particular geographical feature.  
3. **A — courage.** It names an idea or quality rather than a physically observable object.  
4. **B — information.** It is generally noncount; use *some information* or *a piece of information*.  
5. **C — some advice.** *Advice* is normally noncount in standard edited English.  
6. **B — cities.** A consonant followed by *y* usually changes to *ies*.  
7. **C — boxes.** Nouns ending in *x* usually add *-es*.  
8. **B — children.** This is an irregular plural.  
9. **B — women.** *Woman* has the irregular plural *women*.  
10. **B — the student's notebook.** Add apostrophe + *s* to a singular noun.  
11. **C — the employees' schedules.** For a regular plural ending in *s*, place the apostrophe after the *s*.  
12. **A — manager.** The manager performs the action; *the* is a determiner.  
13. **C — proposal.** It receives the action of reviewing.  
14. **C — subject complement.** It follows linking verb *became* and identifies the subject.  
15. **B — Monday.** Days of the week are capitalized.  
16. **B — many applications.** *Applications* is a plural count noun.  
17. **C — three pieces of equipment.** *Equipment* is normally noncount; *pieces* makes the quantity countable.  
18. **A — desk; patience.** A desk is a physical object; patience is an abstract quality.  
19. **C — customers.** The sentence needs the plural noun, not a possessive form.  
20. **A — The information is in the folder.** *Information* is noncount and takes singular agreement here.

## Chapter 3 Quiz: Pronouns

### Questions

1. In *Ari submitted the report because it was due*, what is the antecedent of *it*?  
   A. Ari  
   B. submitted  
   C. report  
   D. due

2. Choose the correct subject pronoun: ____ and I will lead the discussion.  
   A. Her  
   B. She  
   C. Hers  
   D. Herself

3. Choose the correct object pronoun: The instructor emailed Jordan and ____.  
   A. I  
   B. me  
   C. my  
   D. mine

4. Choose the correct pronoun: The final decision is ____.  
   A. their  
   B. them  
   C. theirs  
   D. they

5. Choose the correct pronoun: The team, acting as a single unit, prepared ____ for the presentation.  
   A. itself  
   B. themselves  
   C. them  
   D. theirs

6. In *I myself checked the figures*, *myself* is used as which kind of pronoun?  
   A. interrogative  
   B. intensive  
   C. demonstrative  
   D. indefinite

7. Choose the correct demonstrative pronoun for two nearby forms: ____ are ready to submit.  
   A. This  
   B. That  
   C. These  
   D. It

8. Which word is an interrogative pronoun in *Who called the office?*  
   A. Who  
   B. called  
   C. the  
   D. office

9. Which is an indefinite pronoun?  
   A. those  
   B. someone  
   C. which  
   D. ourselves

10. Choose the correct pronoun: Each applicant should attach ____ résumé.  
    A. their  
    B. them  
    C. theirs  
    D. themselves

11. Which sentence uses singular *they* clearly and correctly?  
    A. Every participant should bring their identification.  
    B. Every participant should bring them identification.  
    C. Every participant should bring theirs identification.  
    D. Every participant should bring themselves identification.

12. Choose the correct pronoun: The supervisor spoke with Lena and ____.  
    A. I  
    B. me  
    C. mine  
    D. myself

13. Choose the correct form in a formal sentence: To ____ should I address the letter?  
    A. who  
    B. whom  
    C. whose  
    D. whoever

14. Choose the correct form in ordinary conversation: ____ did you invite?  
    A. Who  
    B. Whom  
   C. Whose  
   D. Which's

15. Which sentence has a clear pronoun reference?  
    A. When Maya called Noor, she was already at work.  
    B. The laptop was beside the printer, but it was broken.  
    C. Omar sent the draft to Lee, who reviewed it.  
    D. The report and the chart were updated, and they was saved.

16. Which revision removes the vague pronoun? *The committee spoke with the director after it received the complaint.*  
    A. After receiving the complaint, the committee spoke with the director.  
    B. It spoke with it after it received it.  
    C. The committee spoke with the director after they received it.  
    D. It spoke with the director after the complaint received it.

17. Choose the correct pronoun: The applicants introduced ____ before the interview.  
    A. themself  
    B. themselves  
    C. theirselves  
    D. them

18. Choose the correct pronoun: The blue folder is ____.  
    A. her  
    B. hers  
    C. she  
    D. herself

19. Which sentence avoids an unnecessary reflexive pronoun?  
    A. Please contact myself if you need help.  
    B. Please contact me if you need help.  
    C. Please contact mine if you need help.  
    D. Please contact I if you need help.

20. In *Those belong to us*, which word is the object of the preposition *to*?  
    A. Those  
    B. belong  
    C. to  
    D. us

### Answer key

1. **C — report.** *It* refers to the report.  
2. **B — She.** The pronoun is part of the compound subject of *will lead*.  
3. **B — me.** The pronoun is the object of *emailed*.  
4. **C — theirs.** A possessive pronoun can stand alone without a following noun.  
5. **A — itself.** *Team* is treated as one unit in this sentence.  
6. **B — intensive.** *Myself* emphasizes the subject *I*.  
7. **C — These.** *Forms* is plural, and *these* indicates nearby things.  
8. **A — Who.** It asks which person performed the action.  
9. **B — someone.** It refers to a nonspecific person.  
10. **A — their.** Singular *they* and its forms are standard; *their* modifies *résumé*.  
11. **A — Every participant should bring their identification.** Singular *they* can refer to an individual when gender is unknown or irrelevant.  
12. **B — me.** The pronoun is an object of the preposition *with*.  
13. **B — whom.** It is the object of the preposition *to* in this formal construction.  
14. **A — Who.** *Who* is common in everyday questions, including object position.  
15. **C — Omar sent the draft to Lee, who reviewed it.** *Who* clearly refers to Lee, and *it* refers to the draft.  
16. **A — After receiving the complaint, the committee spoke with the director.** The revision removes the unclear *it* and preserves the intended sequence.  
17. **B — themselves.** The plural antecedent *applicants* takes the plural reflexive form.  
18. **B — hers.** The possessive pronoun stands alone.  
19. **B — Please contact me if you need help.** *Me* is the object form required after *contact*; *myself* is not needed.  
20. **D — us.** *Us* is the object of the preposition *to*.

## Chapter 4 Quiz: Adjectives

### Questions

1. Which word is an adjective in *The quiet office faces the courtyard*?  
   A. quietly  
   B. office  
   C. quiet  
   D. faces

2. Which sentence places an adjective after a linking verb?  
   A. The room seems quiet.  
   B. The quiet room is upstairs.  
   C. She spoke quietly.  
   D. They entered the room.

3. In *They bought a small wooden table*, which word is an adjective describing *table*?  
   A. bought  
   B. a  
   C. wooden  
   D. they

4. Choose the natural adjective order:  
   A. a wooden small table  
   B. a small wooden table  
   C. a table wooden small  
   D. a small table wooden

5. Choose the comparative form of *careful*.  
   A. carefuler  
   B. more careful  
   C. most careful  
   D. carefulest

6. Choose the superlative form of *bright*.  
   A. more bright  
   B. brighter  
   C. brightest  
   D. most brightest

7. Choose the correct comparative: This route is ____ than the old one.  
   A. good  
   B. better  
   C. best  
   D. more better

8. Choose the correct superlative: Of the three proposals, this one is the ____.  
   A. more practical  
   B. practicalest  
   C. most practical  
   D. most practicaller

9. Which sentence uses a participial adjective?  
   A. The instructions were confusing.  
   B. They are confusing the instructions.  
   C. They confused the instructions yesterday.  
   D. They will confuse the instructions.

10. In *The audience was excited*, what does *excited* describe?  
    A. the action *was*  
    B. the audience's state  
    C. the speaker's manner  
    D. the time of the event

11. Choose the adjective to complete the sentence: The soup tastes ____.  
    A. well  
    B. good  
    C. wonderfully  
    D. carefully

12. Choose the adverb to complete the sentence: She performed the song ____.  
    A. beautiful  
    B. beautifully  
    C. beauty  
    D. more beautiful

13. Which sentence is correct?  
    A. He feels badly about the mistake.  
    B. He feels bad about the mistake.  
    C. He feels badily about the mistake.  
    D. He feels worst about the mistake than before.

14. Choose the correct adjective: We need a ____ solution than the current one.  
    A. more simple  
    B. simplest  
    C. simpler  
    D. simplely

15. Which sentence has a comparison error?  
    A. This draft is clearer than the first draft.  
    B. This is the clearest draft in the folder.  
    C. This draft is more clearer than the first draft.  
    D. This draft is less clear than the first draft.

16. Which word is the adjective in *The blue and gray banner hung in the lobby*?  
    A. banner  
    B. hung  
    C. lobby  
    D. gray

17. Choose the correct form: The new system is ____ than the previous one.  
    A. efficient  
    B. more efficient  
    C. most efficient  
    D. efficiently

18. Which sentence uses an adjective to describe a noun?  
    A. The speaker answered clearly.  
    B. The clear answer helped the group.  
    C. The speaker answered more clearly.  
    D. The group listened carefully.

19. Choose the correct form: That was the ____ presentation of the day.  
    A. more engaging  
    B. engaginger  
    C. most engaging  
    D. most more engaging

20. Which revision is more precise and avoids unnecessary intensifiers? Original: *The report was really very extremely useful.*  
    A. The report was useful.  
    B. The report was really very extremely useful indeed.  
    C. The report was very, really, extremely useful.  
    D. The report was useful and extremely very really.

### Answer key

1. **C — quiet.** It describes the noun *office*.  
2. **A — The room seems quiet.** *Quiet* follows linking verb *seems* and describes the subject.  
3. **C — wooden.** It describes the noun *table*; *small* is also an adjective, but only *wooden* appears as an answer choice.  
4. **B — a small wooden table.** This is the natural order for these size and material adjectives.  
5. **B — more careful.** Longer adjectives commonly use *more* for the comparative.  
6. **C — brightest.** The short adjective *bright* commonly takes *-est*.  
7. **B — better.** *Better* is the irregular comparative form of *good*.  
8. **C — most practical.** A superlative compares one item with a group; do not double-mark it.  
9. **A — The instructions were confusing.** *Confusing* describes the instructions; in the other choices, the forms are part of verb phrases.  
10. **B — the audience's state.** *Excited* is a participial adjective describing the audience.  
11. **B — good.** Linking verb *tastes* takes an adjective describing the soup.  
12. **B — beautifully.** The adverb describes how she performed.  
13. **B — He feels bad about the mistake.** Here *feels* is a linking verb, so the adjective *bad* describes the subject.  
14. **C — simpler.** The sentence calls for the comparative form of *simple*.  
15. **C — more clearer.** It marks the comparative twice; use *clearer* or *more clear*.  
16. **D — gray.** It describes the noun *banner*. (*Blue* is also an adjective in the sentence.)  
17. **B — more efficient.** A longer adjective commonly uses *more* in the comparative.  
18. **B — The clear answer helped the group.** *Clear* describes the noun *answer*.  
19. **C — most engaging.** This is the superlative form of the longer adjective *engaging*.  
20. **A — The report was useful.** Removing stacked intensifiers makes the description more direct; a specific detail could make it more informative.

## Chapter 5 Quiz: Verbs

### Questions

1. Which word is the main verb in *The coordinator has sent the schedule*?  
   A. coordinator  
   B. has  
   C. sent  
   D. the

2. In *They will be presenting at noon*, which words are helping verbs?  
   A. will and be  
   B. be and presenting  
   C. they and will  
   D. presenting only

3. Choose the correct third-person singular present form: My colleague ____ at the downtown office.  
   A. work  
   B. works  
   C. working  
   D. worked

4. Choose the simple past form of *plan*.  
   A. planed  
   B. planned  
   C. plans  
   D. planning

5. Choose the past participle of *take*.  
   A. took  
   B. taking  
   C. taken  
   D. takes

6. Choose the correct form: We have ____ the final draft.  
   A. wrote  
   B. writing  
   C. written  
   D. writes

7. Which sentence uses the present progressive?  
   A. They review the applications every week.  
   B. They reviewed the applications yesterday.  
   C. They are reviewing the applications now.  
   D. They have reviewed the applications.

8. Choose the correct verb phrase: At 10 a.m. yesterday, I ____ with a client.  
   A. speak  
   B. was speaking  
   C. have spoken  
   D. will speak

9. Which sentence uses the present perfect?  
   A. She has completed the training.  
   B. She completed the training last week.  
   C. She is completing the training now.  
   D. She will complete the training tomorrow.

10. Choose the correct verb phrase: By the time the meeting began, the team ____ the room.  
    A. prepares  
    B. has prepared  
    C. had prepared  
    D. will prepare

11. Choose the correct modal form: You ____ save a copy before editing the file. (advice)  
    A. should  
    B. should to  
    C. shoulds  
    D. should saving

12. Which modal most clearly expresses ability?  
    A. can  
    B. must  
    C. should  
    D. may

13. Which sentence is a polite request?  
    A. Could you send me the updated schedule?  
    B. You must send me the updated schedule.  
    C. You sent me the updated schedule.  
    D. You should send the updated schedule yesterday.

14. Which sentence is in the passive voice?  
    A. The assistant filed the documents.  
    B. The documents were filed by the assistant.  
    C. The assistant is filing the documents.  
    D. The assistant will file the documents.

15. Choose the correct passive form: The application ____ by the committee yesterday.  
    A. reviewed  
    B. was reviewed  
    C. has review  
    D. was reviewing

16. Which sentence uses a transitive verb with a direct object?  
    A. The audience laughed.  
    B. The package arrived.  
    C. The technician repaired the monitor.  
    D. The children slept.

17. Which sentence uses an intransitive verb?  
    A. She opened the window.  
    B. The guests arrived.  
    C. He submitted the form.  
    D. We discussed the proposal.

18. Choose the correct verb form: They ____ the results at yesterday's meeting.  
    A. present  
    B. presents  
    C. presented  
    D. have present

19. Which sentence maintains consistent tense for a sequence of past actions?  
    A. She opened the file, checks the figures, and saved it.  
    B. She opens the file, checked the figures, and saves it.  
    C. She opened the file, checked the figures, and saved it.  
    D. She had opened the file, checks the figures, and will save it.

20. Choose the sentence with a complete verb phrase:  
    A. The team reviewing the proposal now.  
    B. The team is reviewing the proposal now.  
    C. The team review the proposal now yesterday.  
    D. The team has review the proposal now.

### Answer key

1. **C — sent.** *Sent* is the main verb; *has* is the helping verb.  
2. **A — will and be.** The full verb phrase is *will be presenting*; *presenting* is the main verb's *-ing* form.  
3. **B — works.** A singular third-person subject takes *-s* in the simple present.  
4. **B — planned.** A one-syllable verb ending in consonant-vowel-consonant commonly doubles its final consonant before *-ed*.  
5. **C — taken.** *Taken* is the past participle; *took* is the simple past.  
6. **C — written.** The present perfect uses *have* + past participle.  
7. **C — They are reviewing the applications now.** The present progressive is *am/is/are* + *-ing*.  
8. **B — was speaking.** The past progressive describes an action in progress at a particular past time.  
9. **A — She has completed the training.** Present perfect uses *has* + past participle.  
10. **C — had prepared.** Past perfect marks the action completed before another past event.  
11. **A — should.** A modal is followed by the base verb: *should save*.  
12. **A — can.** *Can* commonly expresses ability.  
13. **A — Could you send me the updated schedule?** *Could you* is a conventional polite request form.  
14. **B — The documents were filed by the assistant.** The subject receives the action; the form is *be* + past participle.  
15. **B — was reviewed.** The passive simple past uses *was/were* + past participle.  
16. **C — The technician repaired the monitor.** *Repaired* takes the direct object *monitor*.  
17. **B — The guests arrived.** *Arrived* has no direct object in this sentence.  
18. **C — presented.** *Yesterday's meeting* places the event in the past.  
19. **C — She opened the file, checked the figures, and saved it.** All three verbs describe past actions and use the simple past.  
20. **B — The team is reviewing the proposal now.** It includes a subject and a complete present progressive verb phrase.
